A Japanese-magazine-inspired editorial cover, vertical 3:4 aspect ratio for mobile reading. Strong masthead typography, warm cream paper, vivid red accent, mustard yellow highlight.

**Background**: warm cream paper color #F4EFE6, overlaid with a very fine subtle grey dot grid pattern (like newsprint texture), low opacity. No gradient.

**Top of canvas**:
- A small red rectangular badge in vermillion red #DD3018, white text inside, sans-serif small caps:
  `STUDY MAG · 学習マガジン`
- Aligned to the upper-left, about 30% of canvas width.

**Upper-right small text** (sans-serif, dark grey #555):
`STUDY MAG · 2026-06-02`

**Below the badge, a thick solid black horizontal line** (about 4px) spanning most of the canvas width.

**Main headline area** (centered vertically, left-aligned, generous left margin):
- A large bold red letter "NEW" in vermillion #DD3018, bold sans-serif heavy weight, about 80px tall, positioned at the start of the headline row.
- Immediately to the right, in massive bold black sans-serif (Heavy weight, like Source Han Sans Heavy), the Chinese characters:
  做了个小工具
- Headline characters are very large, very bold, very black.

**Subtitle (below headline, smaller sans-serif, charcoal #2C2C2A, medium weight)**:
自留地 · 给自己当第二海马体用

**Lede block** (below subtitle, with extra whitespace):
- Small label, sans-serif, dark grey: `导读 / LEDE`
- Below the label, a thin black underline running the length of the label.
- Below the underline, body text in dark charcoal sans-serif (medium weight):
  把每天刷到的好文章 扔进去 让它自己整理 再讲给我听
- IMPORTANT: behind the four characters "第二海马体" appears a horizontal mustard yellow #F5C842 highlighter brush stroke (just behind those characters, like a real highlighter). This is the only yellow highlight on the page.

**Bottom of canvas, footer text** (small sans-serif, light grey):
BY ME · 仅供自用 · FOR PERSONAL USE

**Strictly forbidden**: no gradients, no drop shadows, no 3D effects, no cartoon mascot, no emojis, no decorative flowers, no stickers, no wobble lines, no QR code, no logos other than what's described, no exclamation marks.

**Feel**: like the cover of a Japanese 学習マガジン (study magazine), restrained but bold. Quiet self-publication. The product's own visual language.

**Text rendering**: Chinese and Japanese characters must be rendered crisply and accurately. Headline characters must be extremely heavy and bold black. The yellow highlight under "第二海马体" must visibly cover those four characters.
