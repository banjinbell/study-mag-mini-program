**Single accent rule**: ember orange #D97757 only on the small tail segment of the hippocampus. Nowhere else.

**Strictly forbidden**: no cartoon faces, no emojis, no flowers, no stickers, no wobble lines, no textures, no shadows, no gradients, no exclamation marks, no QR code.

**Text rendering**: Chinese characters crisp and accurate. Headline serif, labels and footnote sans-serif.
