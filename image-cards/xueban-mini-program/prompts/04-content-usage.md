A minimal editorial card matching the Claude-aesthetic style of the reference image. Vertical 3:4. Warm cream background #EFE9DF.

**Top-left, headline (Chinese serif)**:
用起来是这样

**Body**: three steps stacked vertically, left-aligned. Each step is a row with:
- A Chinese numeral character ("一" "二" "三") in serif, large, in warm ember orange #D97757
- A space
- A Chinese sentence in sans-serif dark charcoal #2C2C2A

Steps:
一　看到啥扔啥　截图　粘贴　链接都行
二　后台自己处理　OCR　概念抽取　自动归档
三　通勤听　3 分钟速听　章节摘要　详版可选

**Right side decoration**: a thin vertical black ink line (0.5pt) running parallel to the right side of the three numerals, connecting them top-to-bottom. At the bottom end of this vertical line, the line transitions into the anatomical hippocampus shape — the line becomes the start of the hippocampus curve, drawn in thin black ink. The hippocampus sits in the lower-right area.

**Bottom of canvas, footnote (small sans-serif, light grey #999, left-aligned)**:
详细程度可调　已经懂的跳过　看不懂的听详的

**Whitespace**: generous top and right margins, body occupies center-left.

**Single accent rule**: ember orange #D97757 only on the three Chinese numerals 一 二 三. Nowhere else.

**Strictly forbidden**: no cartoon mascot, no emojis, no flowers, no stickers, no wobble lines, no textures, no shadows, no gradients, no exclamation marks, no QR code.

**Text rendering**: Chinese characters crisp and accurate. Headline serif, steps sans-serif body, numerals serif.
